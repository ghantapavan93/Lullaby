Independent engineering prototype. Synthetic child records. Simulated payments. Not affiliated with Nanit.

Privacy

This is a research prototype, not a service. It holds no real people's data.

What it stores

  • Synthetic households, caregivers, a synthetic child and an invented night, created by the demo seed.
  • Actions taken in the demo (corrections, acknowledgments, simulated purchases), in PostgreSQL. On the hosted copy each visitor gets a private workspace with its own databases, wiped when the workspace ends.
  • On the hosted copy, one cookie for that workspace (pl_ws): a random identifier, HttpOnly, that ends with the workspace (after a few idle minutes, or under an hour in all). The server keeps only a hash of it.
  • Product analytics with allow-listed values only (counts, flags and identifiers). Free text is rejected.
  • Your theme choice in this browser's local storage.
  • One short-lived cookie, only when you open a clip in Recorded evidence: it lets this browser fetch that one clip for five minutes. It is HttpOnly, sent only to that clip's address on this site, and holds no personal data.
  • The demo clip is a generated staged clip, or, on the author's machine only, a public-domain (CC0) sample kept out of the published copy. Its audio is removed when it is stored. Nothing is uploaded from your device unless you use the local development import.
  • The hosted copy runs on Vercel (pages) and Render (backend), which keep their usual request logs.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details.

What it never does

  • Contact Nanit's systems, a payment provider, or any third-party analytics service.
  • Accept card details. Every payment is simulated.

A privacy page alone does not make any system compliant with any law; this one only describes the prototype.
