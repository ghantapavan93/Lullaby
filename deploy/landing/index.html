"I paid for this. Why can't I use it?"

A working backend that answers that question one camera at a time. It separates what was bought, where it's connected, which plan the camera has and what actually works, then checks it, and never sells a plan to fix a problem a plan can't fix.

What to try first

  1. Scenario Studio → "Paid, not connected". A confirmed purchase isn't on a camera yet. Connect it without paying again, then watch access get checked as a separate step.
  2. Same symptom, two causes. Two situations where a parent sees the same thing. The real backend decides and refuses differently for each, in a rolled-back transaction.
  3. "Renewal, then recorded evidence". A clip is refused until the store's renewal reaches the same camera. A worker then decodes and measures it. A caregiver's report sits beside the measurement and never changes it.
  4. For engineers → X-Ray and Growth Lab. The trace of each answer, the stored rows behind it, and certification against the test report for this exact code.

What is real, and what isn't

Real
The backend: PostgreSQL, the entitlement rules, the outbox worker, crash recovery, idempotency, and video decoding and measurement (PyAV and numpy).
Synthetic
Every family, child, camera and purchase. Payments and app-store events are simulated; no money moves.
Not claimed
Sleep, breathing, health or safety from video. The clip is generated shapes, with no child in it.
Not Nanit
An independent prototype that uses only Nanit's public product rules. No Nanit code, data, images or branding.

Your workspace is private

Opening the prototype gives your browser its own copy of the synthetic data. Nobody else sees or changes it, and you can't change anyone else's. It ends after 30 idle minutes or 120 minutes in all, and is then wiped. Uploads are off.

This copy runs on free infrastructure: one private workspace at a time, and the backend naps when nobody is using it. If it's busy, try again in a few minutes.
